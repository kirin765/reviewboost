## Codebase Patterns
- For webhook-retry-safe billing logic, normalize all incoming temporal fields at the boundary (utility function) before persistence/comparison.
- When DB ordering may be impacted by null/invalid values, apply deterministic in-memory sort with explicit tie-breakers to keep plan resolution stable under repeated events.
- Unit-test Supabase query/upsert flows with lightweight chainable mocks to validate conflict keys and payload normalization without requiring a real DB.
- For cryptographic webhook verification, validate signature header shape before HMAC work and compare decoded bytes in constant time; this avoids malformed-header edge cases and keeps failures deterministic.
